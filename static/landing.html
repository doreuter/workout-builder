From download to your first pedal stroke

.zwo Ride it on Zwift

Shortcut in Chrome or Edge: click Send to Zwift in VibeGrind and pick your Zwift folder once. From then on, workouts land there directly; skip to step 4.

  1. Download for Zwift in VibeGrind. You get a .zwo file.
  2. On the computer you've used Zwift on, open your Zwift workouts folder:
    • Mac: Finder → Documents/Zwift/Workouts/
    • Windows: Documents\Zwift\Workouts\
    Inside is a folder named with a number: your Zwift ID.
  3. Move the .zwo file into that numbered folder.
  4. Start (or restart) Zwift and go to Training → Workouts → Custom Workouts. Your workout is there.

Ride on iPad, Apple TV or phone? Add the file on a Mac or PC once and open Zwift there: your custom workouts then sync to your other devices automatically.

.fit Ride it on your Garmin

  1. Download for Garmin in VibeGrind. You get a .fit file.
  2. Connect your Garmin watch or Edge bike computer to your computer with its USB cable.
  3. Open the device's storage and copy the file into the GARMIN/NewFiles folder.
  4. Eject the device and unplug it. It imports the workout on its own.
  5. On the device, open Training → Workouts, pick the workout and start riding. It guides you step by step with target power.

Device doesn't show up on your Mac? Many newer Garmin watches connect via MTP, which macOS doesn't open on its own. A free tool like OpenMTP lets you copy the file. Set your FTP on the device so the power targets match.
