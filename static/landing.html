How it works

1

Describe it

Type the session the way you'd tell a friend: “VO2 max, 5×4 min, warm up well.” Change it the same way: “make the recoveries shorter.”

2

See it

VibeGrind draws the workout by training zone, with duration, intensity factor and TSS, and shows watts from your FTP.

3

Ride it

Download it for Zwift (.zwo) or your Garmin (.fit), or import workouts you already have and edit them.

Built around how ready you are

Readiness, explained

Connect Garmin to see a daily readiness estimate from your overnight HRV, sleep, resting heart rate, Body Battery and training load, with the reason behind every number.

Suggestions that fit the day

Ask “what should I ride today?” and get a session that matches your readiness and recent load: intervals when you're fresh, easy spinning when you're not.

Works with your tools

Zwift and Garmin workout files today. Sending workouts straight to Garmin devices is planned.

Your data, your call

Health data is personal. VibeGrind only reads your Garmin data after you connect your account and agree, and only to show your readiness and tailor your workouts.

  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
  • Garmin data is used only for your own dashboard and suggestions.
  • You can disconnect Garmin and have your data deleted at any time.
  • Readiness is an estimate for training, not medical advice.
