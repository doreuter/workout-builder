Data about your heart rate, HRV, sleep and similar measurements can be health data under Art. 9 GDPR. We only process it with your explicit consent, given when you connect Garmin. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see section 7); this doesn't affect processing that happened before.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles beyond what you see in the app. The readiness score and workout suggestions are automated, but they have no legal or similarly significant effect on you (Art. 22 GDPR); they're training suggestions you're free to ignore.

3. AI processing

When you send a chat message, the message, the current workout, your training settings and, if you've connected Garmin, a short summary of your readiness and recent activities are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to create or change the workout. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf as a processor. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models. Please don't put information in the chat that isn't needed for your training.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, settings, workouts and chats: until you delete them or your account.
  • Garmin data: until you disconnect Garmin or delete your account; then it's deleted from our database within 30 days.
  • Usage counters: up to 13 months, for cost tracking.
  • Server logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them, at most [e.g. 30 days, per Render's log retention].

6. Cookies

We use one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It's strictly necessary for the service, so it doesn't need consent (§ 25(2) TDDDG). We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

You can withdraw your consent to Garmin data processing at any time, by disconnecting Garmin or by emailing us. To exercise any of these rights, write to [contact email].

8. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and access to the service is invite-only during the beta.
